How to use Study Sprout

1

Paint your week

On My week, show when you can and can’t study. This is your usual week, and it repeats for every week of the plan.

  • Pick the Free brush and drag over the hours you could study.
  • Pick Busy, type what it is (School, Football, Tuition) and drag over those hours.
  • Blank hours are treated as off-limits, like sleep or downtime.
  • Drag over a painted slot with the same brush to erase it, or use Erase.
  • Copy Monday to Tue–Fri saves time if your school days look alike.
  • On a phone, scroll by dragging the time column on the left, since dragging the grid paints it.
2

Add your subjects

On My subjects, list what you need to cover.

  • Add each subject, then its portions: chapters, topics or past papers, with a rough number of hours.
  • Set how hard the subject is. Harder subjects get a little more priority.
  • Add an exam date if there is one. That subject is planned before exam day, and a countdown appears.
  • Favourite times lock a subject to a time, like Maths every Saturday at 11:30 am. A heart shows on those sessions. The time needs to be free in My week.
  • Tap the coloured blob to change the subject’s sticker colour.
3

Grow your timetable

On My timetable, choose your settings and press Grow my timetable.

  • Session length is how long each study block lasts, and Most study in a day stops days getting overloaded.
  • Breaks leave 30 minutes between sessions. Revision fills spare sessions once portions are covered.
  • Growing again rebuilds the plan from scratch. Moves and rescues are reset, but ticked sessions stay ticked when they land in the same place.
4

Check in on Today

Today is the page to open each day.

  • It lists today’s sessions in order with your busy blocks, with the next one marked Up next.
  • Exam countdown cards show how many days are left and how many sessions you’ve done for each subject.
  • Ticking Done pops up a little encouraging note.
5

Move sessions around

  • On My timetable, pick a week, then a day tab to see that day's sessions. Each tab shows how many sessions are done, like 1/3.
  • To move a session, grab the dotted handle on its sticker and drag it onto another day's tab or onto another session.
  • Or tap the handle, switch to another day or week, then tap the day or a session to put it there.
  • Dropping on a day puts it in the first free gap that day. Dropping on another session swaps the two.
  • Sessions can’t be moved onto or past their subject’s exam day.
6

Rescue missed sessions and print

  • Unticked sessions from the past are marked Missed. Press Rescue to move one to the next free gap, or Rescue all.
  • Rescues stay within your daily limit where possible and always before the exam.
  • Printable version on My timetable gives a clean one-week-per-page layout with tick boxes.
  • Press Theme at the top to switch between the Main theme, Spring, Fall, Winter and Summer. Each has its own colours, sticker shades and drifting doodles.
  • Everything saves in this browser automatically, so you can close the page and come back.
